## `05_1_meta_analysis.R`

**Figures**: none

**Tables** (`results/tables/integrated/`):

- `meta_DE_input_long.tsv` — long-format DE input (gene × dataset × log2FC × SE)
- `meta_DE_input_wide.tsv` — wide-format (one row per gene, one column per dataset)
- `meta_DE_random_effects.tsv` — full REML random-effects results (all genes)
- `meta_DE_meta_up.tsv` — significant upregulated meta-genes (FDR < 0.05)
- `meta_DE_meta_down.tsv` — significant downregulated meta-genes (FDR < 0.05)
- `meta_DE_meta_rank_z.tsv` — gene ranking by z-score (input for fgsea)
- `meta_DE_meta_rank_beta.tsv` — gene ranking by beta (log2FC)
- `meta_DE_I2_summary.tsv` — I² distribution summary
- `meta_DE_k_distribution.tsv` — number of datasets per gene
- `meta_gene_concordance.tsv` — direction concordance scores across datasets
- `meta_core_highconf.tsv` — high-confidence core (FDR < 0.05 + I² < 50%)
- `meta_sig_heterogeneous.tsv` — significant but heterogeneous genes (I² ≥ 50%)
- `meta_DE_REML_warnings_genes.tsv` *(optional — written only if REML convergence warnings occur)*

## `10_fig5_meta.R`

**Figures** (`results/figures/fig5/`):

- `Fig5.pdf` — publication figure (200 × 220 mm)
- `Fig5.png` — 600 DPI raster

**Layout**: Row 1 (57%): Panel A (meta-volcano, beta_meta vs -log10(FDR), 20 lollipop genes labeled) | Panel B (high-confidence gene lollipop, k=3 I²<50%, top 10 UP + 10 DOWN). Row 2 (43%): Panel C (meta-GSEA Hallmarks full width, ranked by beta_meta, 8 pathways FDR < 0.05).

**Note**: meta-GSEA uses `beta_meta` ranking (from `metaGSEA_HALLMARKS_BETA.tsv`), not `z_meta`. The z_meta ranking yields only 1 significant Hallmark due to high heterogeneity (I²=96%); beta_meta captures the average direction of effect and gives 8 pathways at FDR < 0.05.
